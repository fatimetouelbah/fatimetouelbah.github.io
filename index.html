I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Sciences Po Paris, supervised by Clément de Chaisemartin.

My dissertation is carried out under a COFRA (Convention de formation par la recherche en administration) agreement between the Department of Economics at Sciences Po Paris and the DEPP, the statistical service of the French Ministry of National Education.

My research interests are economics of education and impact evaluation of public policies.
